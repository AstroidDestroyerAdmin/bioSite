Always Looking for the Next Adventure

David has a lot of different interests, but many of them have one thing in common: they keep him active, challenged, and learning something new.

Favorite Hobbies

Motorcycles

Riding motorcycles is one of David's favorite ways to get outside and enjoy the open road. It combines adventure, freedom, and the chance to explore new places.

Rock Climbing

Rock climbing gives David a physical and mental challenge. It takes focus, patience, and problem-solving to figure out the best way to reach the top.

Canoeing and the Outdoors

David also enjoys getting out on the water and spending time in nature. Canoeing down the Kern River is the kind of outdoor adventure that gives him a chance to explore and get away from everyday life.

Technology

When he is not outdoors, David enjoys technology and learning how things work. He likes experimenting with computers, solving problems, and building his knowledge of the tech world.
